name: dense
description: Optimise a page with many sections or a lot of data, or ideate one before it is built — cut, order, chunk, summarise, emphasise, and run the four table tasks (NN/g, Formatting Long-Form Content and Data Tables)
---

The page, sheet or idea is `$ARGUMENTS`. Read `.claude/rules/dense-pages.md`,
`.claude/rules/reading.md` and `.claude/rules/feature-value.md`, then the
project's style guide sections on layout, tables, tabs, side sheets and stat
tiles. Then read the page, its components, and its copy strings. If the page
can be run, look at it at a typical desktop width. Otherwise say the review was
from the code.

**If it exists (optimise):**

1. **List everything on the page**: every section, tile, column, pill and
   line. Name the one decision the page serves.
2. **Cut first.** Mark each item essential, granular, condensable and simple.
   Remove or defer what fails. Name columns that repeat a value.
3. **Order.** The answer is above the fold. Columns follow the task. Related
   things sit together.
4. **Chunk and layer.** Headings that lead with the key word, related lists as
   tabs, detail in a sheet, and no accordion hiding what the task needs. More
   than about seven chunks is a finding.
5. **Summary first**, from the server, with its age.
6. **Emphasis budget.** About 30% or less. Short bullets. One or two callouts,
   as panels.
7. **Visuals** carry information or go.
8. **Tables** by find, compare, view, act. Say which of column hiding, batch
   actions and an in-page contents list would help, and whether they are in
   the product.

**If it is an idea (ideate):** follow rule 8. List the items and their
sources, sort into leads, supports, deeper and removed, then offer three shapes
built only from existing components, recommend one, name the first slice, and
say what is untested.

Report before and after for each change with its source (the rule and the NN/g
article), and open questions for the business. Do not change code unless
asked. When fixing, run the project's lint, typecheck and build, and say
plainly if it has not been tried in a browser. Hand rewording to `/copy`.
